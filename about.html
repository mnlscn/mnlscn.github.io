about

From airport security to production AI.

I grew up in Sicily and took a bachelor’s degree in Business Economics. My first job was airport security: three years as a licensed sworn guard at Catania International, trained in counter-terrorism procedures, inside critical infrastructure where every process exists because failure is expensive. Checklists, layered defences, asking “how does this fail?” before “how does this work?”: that discipline is the same one I now apply to AI systems.

In 2021 I went back to university: an M.Sc. in Data Science at the University of Catania, summa cum laude, with an Erasmus semester in AI at Goethe University Frankfurt. Since then: data pipelines and machine learning at Koexai; applied AI at Neodata, including leading an airport media-intelligence platform; and now ADP in Barcelona, on the core team of an enterprise AI platform.

Outside work I contribute to open source, organised and technically led two GenAI hackathons at the University of Catania, and serve as community manager of Applied AI Engineers ↗, a community for engineers moving into forward-deployed AI roles. Current rabbit holes: AI × cybersecurity, AI × music.
